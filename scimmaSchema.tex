\subsection{EFD alert schema}

The EFD alert schema. Note that the \texttt{reward\_map} field is iterated as \texttt{reward\_map0}, \texttt{reward\_map1}, ... \texttt{reward\_map12287}, where each entry is one pixel in a flattened Healpix skymap of $\texttt{nside}=32$ and \texttt{RING} ordering.
